How the monthly close works
  1. By business day 5: upload the documents below for the month that just ended. Each item says who usually has it.
  2. By business day 7: Francisco uploads the trial balance, journal entry report and allocation reports after the month’s entries are in.
  3. By business day 10: Martinez Accounting reconciles every bank account and prepares the month’s correcting entries with support, ready for approval.
  4. By business day 12: Patty, or someone she designates, approves the entries. Proteus posts them and locks the month in AccuFund so the numbers no longer change.
  5. By business day 15: Martinez Accounting sends the one-page grant report and monthly summary to Michelle.

Business days count Monday to Friday after the month ends. Holidays may move a date by a day. Report names under “Where to get it” match the reports Proteus sent us in October. If a name looks different on your screen, upload what you have and add a note.
